The Falling Leaves

Draft story, written to fill this space. Nana's own version is coming.

In the fall, the big tree in the middle of Wonderland lets go of its leaves. They are not ordinary leaves. They are red and gold and orange all at once, with a dark stem like the handle of a teacup, and they come down slowly, turning over and over, as if they were in no hurry at all.

Stormy watches them from the highest branch. Every time one floats past, Stormy reaches out a paw, and almost catches it, and lets it go.

The girl catches them instead. She runs underneath with her bag open, and she keeps only the very best ones: the reddest, the goldest, the ones that still have all their points. The others she leaves for Mudpie, who likes to hide in a pile of leaves until only a spotted tail sticks out.

Bus Driver Pig slows down in leaf season. It is the only time of year he does. He drives right through the biggest piles, very carefully, so the leaves fly up around the red bus like a second, softer kind of weather, and every pig in every window laughs.

And on Thursday, at the Doormouse's tea party, everyone gets a leaf for a saucer. That is the rule. Nobody remembers who made it, but nobody has ever wanted to change it.
