Welcome to Wonderland.

These are the stories I told to one of my granddaughters, and the pictures I drew for her in a little notebook while I told them. Everyone here knows everyone else: the Doormouse in her teacup, two cats who eat only salmon, a dragon too big for the parachute, and a pig who drives much too fast.

Pick a picture to read its story. Draft welcome, until Nana writes her own.
